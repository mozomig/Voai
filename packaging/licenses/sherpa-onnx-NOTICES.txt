The native helper includes sherpa-onnx 2.0.2 (Apache License 2.0).
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/v2.0.2
See sherpa-onnx-LICENSE for the Apache License, Version 2.0.

The optional downloadable models are the INT8 ONNX conversion of pyannote/segmentation-3.0
(MIT; copyright 2022 CNRS) and the 3D-Speaker CAM++ VoxCeleb ONNX embedding model
(Apache License 2.0). The weights are installed separately.
https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-pyannote-segmentation-3-0
https://huggingface.co/pyannote/segmentation-3.0
https://modelscope.cn/models/iic/speech_campplus_sv_en_voxceleb_16k
https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/speaker-embedding-models
See speaker-segmentation-LICENSE and sherpa-onnx-LICENSE.

See onnxruntime-LICENSE and onnxruntime-ThirdPartyNotices.txt.
